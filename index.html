Meet the Chef

Chef Sara Akkar

"I created this brand to give people who don't yet know Moroccan treasures the chance to discover our culture through delicious, nourishing food."

Chef Sara Akkar is a Moroccan chef living in the UAE, with a passion for cooking built over more than 16 years of experience. Raised on the recipes of her own family's kitchen, she has spent her career mastering the techniques that make Moroccan food what it is — patient, generous, and full of flavour.

BY CHEF SARA was born from a simple idea: that the best way to introduce people to Moroccan culture is through its food. Every khlii, every jar of smen, every spice blend she makes is prepared the same way she learned it at home — by hand, in small batches, without shortcuts.

About Us

The Brand By Chef Sara.

BY CHEF SARA exists to bring Morocco's pantry — its preserves, its spice blends, its breakfast table — to anyone in the UAE curious enough to try it.

Why we started

Morocco's food culture rarely gets the spotlight it deserves outside its own borders. BY CHEF SARA was built to change that — one jar, one spice blend, one curious first taste at a time.

Handcrafted, always

Every product is made by hand, in small batches, by Chef Sara herself — never outsourced, never mass-produced.

True to the original

Recipes are kept authentic on purpose — the same taste and texture you'd find in a Moroccan home, not a modernized version of it.

A bridge to Moroccan culture

Every product is a small introduction to Morocco's food culture — for people meeting it for the first time, and for Moroccans missing home.
