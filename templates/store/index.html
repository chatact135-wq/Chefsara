Meet the Chef

Chef Sara Akkar

"I created this brand to give people who don't yet know Moroccan treasures the chance to discover our culture through delicious, nourishing food."

Chef Sara Akkar is a Moroccan chef living in the UAE, with a passion for cooking built over more than 16 years of experience. Raised on the recipes of her own family's kitchen, she has spent her career mastering the techniques that make Moroccan food what it is — patient, generous, and full of flavour.
