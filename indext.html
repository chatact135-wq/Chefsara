Meet The Founder

Chef Sara Akkar

“I created this brand to give people who don't yet know Moroccan treasures the chance to discover our culture through delicious, nourishing food[cite: 1].”

Chef Sara Akkar is a Moroccan chef living in the UAE, with a passion for cooking built over more than 16 years of experience. Raised on the recipes of her own family's kitchen, she has spent her career mastering the techniques that make Moroccan food what it is — patient, generous, and full of flavour[cite: 1].

BY CHEF SARA was born from a simple idea: that the best way to introduce people to Moroccan culture is through its food. Every khlii, every jar of smen, every spice blend she makes is prepared the same way she learned it at home — by hand, in small batches, without shortcuts[cite: 1].

Our Philosophy

The Brand By Chef Sara

Why We Started

Commercial shortcuts dominate modern food production. We chose the opposite path. Every jar represents hours of patient simmering, precise spice curation, and uncompromising dedication to authentic Moroccan culinary heritage. No additives, no factory floors — just pure tradition.
